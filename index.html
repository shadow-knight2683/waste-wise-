How it works

Three steps between confusion and the correct bin.

Built for the two seconds you're standing in front of a row of bins with something in your hand.

From search to sorted

The whole flow takes about four seconds — no account, no app download, no signage to decode.

1Search the item by name, brand, or material.
2Read the disposal method and prep notes.
3Log it and watch your campus total climb.

One database, every material

Recyclables, compostables, hazardous waste and e-waste mapped to the exact bin colours used on your campus — so guidance matches the bins you actually stand in front of.

84% of searched items found in the database

Features

Everything the sign on the bin should have told you.

A focused toolkit for students, hostel wardens and campus sustainability offices.

Instant item search

Battery, plastic bottle or food waste — type it and get the answer without squinting at a poster.

Disposal method

Step-by-step prep: rinse it, tape the terminals, remove the cap, flatten the box.

Community tracking

Every logged item rolls up into a hostel, department or campus total your office can report on.

Contamination alerts
